CLINICAL HISTORY: 68-year-old male presenting with acute dyspnea, non-productive cough, and febrile episodes.
EXAMINATION: Chest Radiograph PA and Lateral views.

FINDINGS:
There is bilateral patchy alveolar opacity noted in the lower lobes, more pronounced on the right side, consistent with consolidation. Minimal subsegmental atelectasis is noted in the left mid-lung zone. 
The cardiomediastinal silhouette reveals mild cardiomegaly. The pleural spaces show evidence of a small left-sided pleural effusion; no evidence of pneumothorax is identified.

IMPRESSION:
1. Patchy bilateral lower lobe consolidation, suspicious for acute infectious etiology (pneumonia).
2. Mild cardiomegaly and a small left pleural effusion.
3. No pneumothorax.
